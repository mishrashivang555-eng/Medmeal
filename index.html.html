People may forget doses—and may not know which food or drinks matter for their medicines.

Complex routines

Opportunity: bring reminders, verified guidance, and simple tracking into one place.

Older adults

Busy working people

  • Fast medicine setup

How it works

A simple four-step user journey

Keep each action clear and avoid asking users to interpret medical information on their own.

1

Add medicines

Enter medicine name, prescribed dose, timing, and verified food instructions.

2

Get reminders

Receive a notification when a scheduled dose is due.

3

Check guidance

See relevant food/drink precautions and safe meal suggestions.

4

Track the day

Record dose status and review the daily summary.

The app supports a prescribed routine; it does not prescribe or change treatment.
Core features

What belongs in the first experience?

Prioritize the features that make the app useful without making it medically risky.

Medication schedule

Medicine list, prescribed dose, and timing.

Reminder notifications

Due-time alerts and clear status options.

Dose tracking

Taken, skipped, or missed—without dose changes.

Food & drink guidance

Reviewed precautions linked to the medicine.

Accessible design

Large text, simple language, and few steps.

Caregiver option

User-controlled support and alerts.

Food guidance

Useful guidance must be trustworthy

Food and medicine advice should come from reviewed sources—not an unrestricted AI guess.

Show verified instructions

  • With food / without food
  • Relevant food or drink interactions
  • Source and last review date

Flag uncertainty

  • Missing or conflicting information
  • Multiple medicines requiring review
  • Refer to a pharmacist or clinician

Personalize carefully

  • Respect allergies and dietary needs
  • Suggest meals within known restrictions
  • Never replace clinical advice
Safety boundary: never tell a user to change a dose or double a missed dose.

Safety & trust

Safety is part of the product—not a later feature

Medication and nutrition guidance needs clear limits, review, and user control.

✓Reviewed content

Use authoritative medicine instructions and qualified review.

✓No dose decisions

Do not recommend starting, stopping, changing, or doubling doses.

✓Multiple medicines

Flag possible interactions for professional review.

✓Privacy by design

Protect health information and get consent for caregiver sharing.

✓Clear escalation

When data is missing, direct users to a pharmacist or clinician.

Success criteria

How will we know the prototype helps?

Test whether it makes the daily routine easier, clearer, and safer.

Usability

Can a new user add a medicine and schedule a reminder without help?

Reliability

Do reminders fire when expected, including after app restarts?

Comprehension

Can users understand food precautions and know when to ask a professional?

Measure task completion, reminder reliability, user understanding, and reported confusion.
